Curb Log

USC · street parking

How much street parking is usually available near an office at USC, by half hour and weekday, from LADOT's curb sensors. Basis is where the places and hours are picked; Pattern is the answer.

About these pages

Made by Citina Liang, a PhD candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering at USC Viterbi who models how people behave and how diseases spread. It started when she missed the deadline for a campus parking pass. Curb Log came first, to learn when the meters near her office have space. Its grid showed the hardest days were when the side streets are swept, which raised the next question: when does enforcement actually come? USC Ticket Clock answers that from the city's own tickets, and LA Street Rules does it for every block in Los Angeles. All from the city's open data, and it updates itself. Built with Claude Code in about a week; the code is on GitHub.
